**1: Student**
* Student logs into the app.
* Selects **year** → options: **10th** or **2nd PU**.
* Then selects **board** → options: **State** or **CBSE** *(if 10th is chosen)*.
* Then selects **course** → options: **PCMB** or **PCMC** *(if 2nd PU is chosen and it is Karnataka State Board)*.
* Then selects **subject** *(required for both 10th and 12th)*.

**Subjects List:**
* For **10th**: Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English
* For **12th (PCMB)**: Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology
* For **12th (PCMC)**: Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Computer Science

**Next Options:**
* Option to **View Previous Year Papers**
* Option to **Create Question Paper**

**If “View Previous Year Questions” is selected:**
* A **PDF of previous year questions** appears.
* Questions are arranged in **descending order** based on how many times they have appeared.
* Use **semantic analyzer** to detect same-meaning but differently worded into questions [combine same question asked into differtent year into a single question from the uploaded pdfs] .
* Each question shows a **year stamp** on the **top right corner** indicating when it appeared.
* Students can **download these PDFs**, but:
  * They can **download only 2 PDFs for free**.
  * After that, each additional PDF costs **₹40**.
* There are **two separate download counters** — one for previous year papers and one for other question papers.

**If “Create Question Paper” is selected:**
* The question paper follows the **same format** as the uploaded ones.
* Students can choose **difficulty level**:
  * **Easy** → mostly repeated questions.
  * **Medium** → mix of frequently and moderately repeated questions.
  * **Hard** → mix of moderately and rarely repeated questions.
